# Wiki

1. **Memory first.** The memory skill's `search` covers the lessons, the wiki and the knowledge base together; run it first on a task.
2. **Then the wiki itself** when you need more of it: `search` with a few words or a page title gives up to 10 pages and the best paragraph of the top one. `list` is every page title, for finding the right guide.
3. **Read the page** at the path `search` prints, then check what it says against this machine before acting: the wiki says how things are meant to work, this machine says how they are.
4. **Cite** the page title and address when you use it.
